index. Each import checked the product's own CC0 field, the included License.txt,
archive and preview checksums, observed formats and publisher-listed file count.

supported licensed preview input. Existing checks were preserved rather than
stamped as new. The large one-off expansion was explicitly requested; normal
scheduled runs retain the five-addition cap.

sale prices, USD currency, applicable commercial licensing, ratings and absolute
marketplace Offer.priceValidUntil evidence. A character pack with an unclear
original paid price and an SFX pack whose full commercial terms were unavailable
were excluded. Paid archives and publisher marketing art were not copied.
